How to read this grid & references

Both ла́зить and ла́зать are multidirectional climbing verbs. Their first-person singular forms are ла́жу and ла́заю, respectively; both are shown in the same cell.

The pronoun selector chooses the example subject for finite forms and is hidden for infinitives. Past agrees in number and gender, not person, so it can use я or ты too. Present and future allow all three persons; their gender comes from the pronoun and does not change the verb ending, so the gender controls appear only for singular past forms. Number is shared across tenses; gender is shared across past singular and third-person singular. Choosing я or ты preserves a stored masculine/feminine choice, which can be changed independently for their past forms. Neuter singular uses оно́. Hidden selections are retained. A perfective verb has no present tense, so its present cell shows a dash. Future is simple for perfective verbs and буду / бу́дем + infinitive for imperfective verbs. With по-, the right column means doing the activity for a while; it is not the imperfective partner of the left column.

The prefixed columns follow the corresponding base families, including conventional stem changes. A cell is judged in its movement sense; other dictionary meanings do not make it ungrammatical.
